Start work with **Create**. Just say what you want done. It names itself, grows its own icon, and the sidebar self-organizes: a focus agent files it by topic. Bigger sidebar changes arrive as proposals you can accept or keep editing.

![Happy gathers its new-work buttons into one stem cell, then names the work and grows an icon from its first message.](/video/stem-cell.mp4)

**Beta, off by default.** Turn on **Settings → Experimental → Stem cells**. The switch enables the new sidebar, Create and the focus agent together. The focus agent's proposals need Happy Agent **0.4.107 or newer**.
