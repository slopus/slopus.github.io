# New Work Starts as a Stem Cell

Every AI app keeps adding **front doors**: a new chat, a project, an assistant, a mode. Each one asks you to **decide what the work is before you've started it**. In Happy Desktop we're trying **one door** instead: new work starts as a **stem cell**, and you **just say what it is**.

## Every AI app keeps adding ways to start

![A Create button with its Create new menu open, listing Page, Slides, Sheet (coming soon), Site, Image, Folder, Space, Start from template, Upload files and Connect a plugin.](/img/stem-cell-create-menu.webp)

*The Create menu in one AI workspace.*

Ten ways to start, and you haven't said a word yet. You rarely know what the work is until you're in it: the quick question turns into a week of work, and the project turns out to be one question. Pick wrong, and the work stays wherever you started it.

## One button, one input box

![Eight create buttons, New task, +, New tab, New window, New chat, New bot, New todo list and New document, contract into cells and flow into one stem cell, labelled New. Then ping me on serious posthog alerts is typed into the input box below it.](/video/stem-cell/one/)

So start with something that isn't anything yet. **Create, ⌘N and ⌘T** all make the same thing: a row called **New**, with an empty chat that says "Say what it is — it'll find its place." You don't pick a kind, a project or a mode. You type what you want.

## It becomes what you meant

![The stem cell divides into the colours of a red beacon clay icon and settles into its shape, while its label changes from New to PostHog alerts.](/video/stem-cell/grow/)

Send `ping me on serious posthog alerts`. Within a minute the row **names itself**, PostHog alerts, and the cell **grows into its icon**, picked from 136 clay icons. Call it something else and your name sticks.

That's the part we've shipped. Next, a stem cell becomes **the right kind of thing**, opens **the right repositories** to work in, and **loops in the right people**: whoever last worked on that area, or the teammate who already started another take on it.

## The sidebar is the new task tracker

Working with agents feels like throwing boomerangs. You send a bunch out, it's great fun, they fly far, and at some point they all start coming back at you. The sidebar is where they land, so it has to work like a task tracker, not a chat history.

![Happy Desktop's sidebar before: Search, Create, Happy, then four new rows right below Happy, PostHog alerts, Signup funnel, Fix login bug and Q4 board deck, then the Engineering section with Auth refactor and the Board section with Q3 board deck.](/img/stem-cell-sidebar-before.webp "Before") ![The same sidebar after: Search, Create, Happy; Engineering with Auth refactor and Fix login bug; Board with Q3 board deck and Q4 board deck; a new Analytics section with PostHog alerts and Signup funnel; and Distractions with Rewrite the website in Rust.](/img/stem-cell-sidebar-after.webp "After")

A stem cell **puts itself where it belongs**: in the section for its subject, next to related work, the way you've organised things. When several rows share a subject no section fits, a new section appears. And whatever you don't want right now goes into **Distractions**: drop it there, and it waits until you're ready. Happy has a **dedicated focus agent**, Prioritizing, that keeps the sidebar in that shape.

## Try it

Stem cells are experimental and off by default. In Happy Desktop, open Settings, then Experimental, and turn on **Spaces sidebar** and **Stem cells**. It guesses, and you can always correct it.
