## What if… you needed one button?

In Happy, we're trying one door instead:

**All new work starts as a stem cell.**

![Happy's Create button, and below it a highlighted row called New with a pink stem cell as its icon.](/img/stem-cell-new-row.png "row")

Create makes a row called New. You say what it is, and it names itself, grows its own icon and slots into the right section, even if that section is Distractions.

## What happens next?

Once you've said enough, the stem cell **morphs into the right thing**: it finds the repository to work in and loops in the people who should know.

![Happy: Create puts a New row in the slot under Happy. Asked to ping on serious PostHog alerts, the stem cell subdivides into its icon and names itself PostHog alerts. *Naming, icons and sections work today. Repositories and people are next on our roadmap.*

## Every boomerang comes back

With today's models, starting work is cheap, and a little addictive. You throw one boomerang after another: a quick fix, an experiment, a side quest. They fly far, and it's fun. Then they all start coming back at once, and you're overwhelmed.

That's why Happy has a dedicated **focus agent** whose only job is to keep you on point. It files new work next to related work, parks side quests in Distractions, and keeps the sidebar in shape, so when a boomerang comes back, you know where to catch it.
