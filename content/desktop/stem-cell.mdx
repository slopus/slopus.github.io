# New Work Starts as a Stem Cell

New work in Happy Desktop starts as one stem cell. Say what it is, and it names itself and grows into its icon.

## What if there was one?

![What if there was one? The eight buttons, contracting into pink cells where they stand.](/img/stem-cell/02-what-if-one.webp)

Create in the sidebar, ⌘N and ⌘T all make the same thing: a stem cell.

## Enter Stem Cell Mode

![Enter Stem Cell Mode: one pink stem cell, labelled New.](/img/stem-cell/03-stem-cell-mode.webp)

A stem cell is a row labelled "New", right below Happy in the sidebar, with an empty chat. Underneath, it's a fresh task in its own folder, with no project. Its chat says "Say what it is — it'll find its place." Nothing names it, gives it an icon or moves it until you send your first message.

## Just say what it is

![Just say what it is: ping me on serious posthog alerts in the composer, under the stem cell labelled New.](/img/stem-cell/04-say-what-it-is.webp)

Type `ping me on serious posthog alerts` and send it.

## It names itself

![It names itself: the stem cell dividing into the colours of the alerting icon, already titled PostHog alerts.](/img/stem-cell/05-names-itself-dividing.webp)

The chat's own model names the row by its subject in one to three words, at the model's default effort, within a minute: "PostHog alerts". With your first message, Prioritizing, the agent that keeps your sidebar, picks the row's icon from 136 clay icons and sets it right away. It moves the row next to related work later, once you've stepped away.

When the icon arrives, the cell divides into the icon's colours and converges onto its silhouette over 2.5 seconds, drawn by a WebGL2 shader in a worker, then hands over to the plain image. Turn off "Animate new work's icon", or ask your system for reduced motion, and the icon appears at once.

## Or start a bot

A stem cell stays a task. Its agent can message bots but can't create one, so it never turns itself into a bot. When you want a bot, with its own face and one conversation that keeps going, choose New bot under the stem cell's line and pick a face, or ask the [Chief of Staff](/chief-of-staff/) to make one.

## That's it

> Your interface needs one button to start work and one input box. That's it.

## Turn it on

Stem cells are experimental. In [Happy Desktop](/#download), open Settings → Experimental and turn on Spaces sidebar and Stem cells.
