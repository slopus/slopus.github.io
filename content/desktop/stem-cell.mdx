# New Work Starts as a Stem Cell

Every AI app keeps adding **front doors**: a new chat, a project, an assistant, a mode. Each one asks you to **decide what the work is before you've started it**.

## Every AI app keeps adding ways to start

![A Create button with its Create new menu open, listing Page, Slides, Sheet (coming soon), Site, Image, Folder, Space, Start from template, Upload files and Connect a plugin.](/img/stem-cell-create-menu.webp)

*The Create menu in Codex.*

Ten ways to start, and you haven't said a word yet. You rarely know what the work is until you're in it: the quick question turns into a week of work, and the project turns out to be one question. Pick wrong, and the work stays wherever you started it.

## What if you hit one button?

In Happy Desktop we're trying **one door** instead: all new work starts as a **stem cell**.

![A pink stem cell: a soft, irregular membrane around a darker nucleus.](/img/stem-cell.png "small")

Create, ⌘N and ⌘T all make one: a row called **New**, with an empty chat. You say what it is, and it **names itself** and **grows into its icon**.

Next, the stem cell **morphs into the right thing**, finds **the right repositories** to work in, and finds **the people to loop in**: whoever worked on this area before, or the teammate who already started another take on it.
