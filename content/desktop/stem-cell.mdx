# New Work Starts as a Stem Cell

Every AI app keeps adding front doors: a new chat, a project, an assistant, a mode. Each one asks you to decide what the work is before you've started it. In Happy Desktop we're trying one door instead: new work starts as a stem cell, and you just say what it is.

## Every AI app keeps adding ways to start

Chat apps started with one box. Then came a new chat, and a project to put it in. A custom assistant. A canvas. An agent mode, a research mode, a model picker, tabs and windows. Each one is useful on its own. Together they mean that before you type a word, you answer a question you can't answer yet: what kind of thing is this?

![A Create button with its Create new menu open, listing Page, Slides, Sheet (coming soon), Site, Image, Folder, Space, Start from template, Upload files and Connect a plugin.](/img/stem-cell-create-menu.webp)

You usually don't know what the work is when you start. "Look into why signups dropped" might be a quick question, a week of work, or something that should keep watching the numbers every morning. You find out by starting. But every button asks you to commit first, and a wrong guess costs you.

Say you open a quick chat to make sense of one stack trace. Forty messages later it's a migration, and it sits outside every project, so moving it means starting a new chat and pasting the context back in. Or you want to hand over a link and one sentence, and the new-assistant form wants a name, an icon and instructions first.

> so there is no good default. chat is often too limited for real tasks. work is too slow & cumbersome for normal queries. constantly switching between them makes the entire product infinitely more complex.
>
> — signüll, @signulll, [9 Aug 2026](https://x.com/signulll/status/2086586335838842972)

So you pick the nearest button. The sidebar fills with chats called "New chat", work stays wherever it was started, and the sorting is left for later, which means never. What people ask for is the opposite: let the app do the sorting.

## One button, one input box

Almost nobody is asking for more modes. Rabi Shanker Guha put the frustration with today's apps like this:

> Somehow, the smartest software we’ve ever built took us back to a textbox
>
> — Rabi Shanker Guha, @rabi_guha, [15 Oct 2026](https://x.com/rabi_guha/status/2110737805823672518)

He means it as a complaint. We think the box is the right way in. The trouble is the row of doors in front of it, each asking what the work is before you've said. So stop asking at the door: start with something that isn't anything yet, let the person say what they want, and let the work decide what it is from that.

## How a stem cell works in Happy

### It waits for you

Create in the sidebar, ⌘N and ⌘T all make the same thing: a row labelled "New", right below Happy, with an empty chat. Underneath, it's a fresh task in its own folder, with no project. Its chat says "Say what it is — it'll find its place." Nothing names it, gives it an icon or moves it until you send your first message.

### It names itself and grows its icon

Your first message is the whole setup. Within a minute, the chat's own model names the row by its subject in one to three words: send `ping me on serious posthog alerts`, and the row becomes "PostHog alerts". Your second message gets one more look, and the name changes only if the conversation plainly contradicts it. A name you give it by hand is final.

At the same time, Prioritizing, the agent that keeps your sidebar, picks the row's icon from 136 clay icons. The cell divides into the icon's colours and settles into its shape over 2.5 seconds, unless "Animate new work's icon" is off or your system asks for reduced motion.

### It files itself later

New work waits right below Happy while you're using it. Once you've stepped away or the app is out of sight, Prioritizing moves each new row next to related work, into the section for its subject. It adds a section only when several rows share a subject that no section fits. Nothing moves while you're working.

## What we don't know yet

Stem cells are experimental and off by default. A stem cell is always a task, and it stays one: its agent can message bots but can't create one, so it never turns itself into a bot. When you want a bot, with its own face and one conversation that keeps going, choose New bot under the stem cell's line, or ask the [Chief of Staff](/chief-of-staff/) to make one. A todo list, a document and the other kinds are under the same line. Whether one door is enough for everything is what we're finding out.

## Turn it on

In [Happy Desktop](/#download), open Settings, then Experimental, and turn on Spaces sidebar and Stem cells.
