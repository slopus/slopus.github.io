# New Work Starts as a Stem Cell

Every AI app keeps adding front doors: a new chat, a project, an assistant, a mode. Each one asks you to **decide what the work is before you've started it**.

## Every AI app keeps adding ways to start

![A Create button with its Create new menu open, listing Page, Slides, Sheet (coming soon), Site, Image, Folder, Space, Start from template, Upload files and Connect a plugin.](/img/stem-cell-create-menu.webp)

*The Create menu in Codex.*

Ten ways to start, and you haven't said a word yet. You rarely know what the work is until you're in it: the quick question turns into a week of work, and the project turns out to be one question. Pick one, and you may have to reorganise the work later.

If that frustrates you, you're not alone. Plenty of people feel the same way, and so do we.

## What if you hit one button?

In Happy Desktop we're trying **one door** instead: all new work starts as a stem cell.

![Happy Desktop's Create button, and below it a highlighted row called New with a pink stem cell as its icon.](/img/stem-cell-new-row.png "row")

Create makes a row called New. You say what it is, and it names itself, grows its own icon and slots into the right section, even if that section is Distractions.

## What happens next?

Once you've said enough, the stem cell **morphs into the right thing**: it finds the repository to work in and loops in the people who should know.

![Happy Desktop: ask to be pinged on serious PostHog alerts. The New row names itself PostHog alerts, the agent replies with an inline chart of exceptions per hour with a dashed alert line, changes the alerting code, and the row becomes a space holding the chat, the repository it changed, and a chat with Steve (CTO).](/video/stem-cell/app/ "autoplay")

*Naming, icons and sections work today. Repositories and people are next on our roadmap.*
