# New Work Starts as a Stem Cell

New work in Happy Desktop starts as one stem cell. Say what it is, and it names itself, picks its icon and finds its place in the sidebar.

## Every AI app keeps adding ways to start

Chat apps started with one box. Then came a new chat, and a project to put it in. A custom assistant. A canvas. An agent mode, a research mode, a model picker, tabs and windows. Each one is useful on its own. Together they mean that before you type a word, you answer a question you can't answer yet: what kind of thing is this?

You usually don't know. "Look into why signups dropped" might be a quick question, a week of work, or something that should keep watching the numbers every morning. You find out by starting. But every button asks you to commit first, and a wrong guess costs you: the chat that should have been a project, the project that turned out to be one question, the assistant you set up for a job you did once.

So you pick the nearest button. The sidebar fills with chats called "New chat", work stays wherever it was started, and the sorting is left for later, which means never.

Our own app is no better. Happy Desktop has New task, a + for a new tab, New bot, and a create menu with a todo list, a document, a calendar, a system monitor and a shared chat: each a different kind of thing, each a decision made at the door.

## One button, one input box

The fix is to stop asking at the door. Start with something that isn't anything yet, let the person say what they want, and let the work decide what it is from that.

![Before and after. Before: decide what it is, then start. Six boxes: New task, New tab, New chat, New bot, New document, New todo list. Each one is a kind of thing, and you pick before you have said a word about the work. Now: start, then say what it is. A pink stem cell labelled New, an arrow marked say it, and a row titled PostHog alerts with its icon: named, with an icon, filed later. One button and one input box; the work takes its name, icon and place from you.](/img/stem-cell-doors.svg)

In Happy Desktop that something is a stem cell: an unnamed row in the sidebar with an empty chat. Create in the sidebar, ⌘N and ⌘T all make one.

## How a stem cell works in Happy

### It waits for you

A stem cell is a row labelled "New", right below Happy in the sidebar. Underneath, it's a fresh task in its own folder, with no project. Its chat says "Say what it is — it'll find its place." Nothing names it, gives it an icon or moves it before your first message.

### You say what it is

Type what you want, the way you'd say it: `ping me on serious posthog alerts`. That message is the whole setup.

### It names itself

The chat's own model reads your first message and names the row by its subject, in one to three words, at the model's default effort, within a minute. For that message, Opus 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5 both named it "PostHog alerts". Your second message gets one more look, and the name changes only if the conversation plainly contradicts it. A name you give it by hand is final.

### It picks its icon

At the same moment, Prioritizing, the agent that keeps your sidebar, picks the row's icon from 136 clay icons and sets it right away. For PostHog alerts it picks `alerting`, a red beacon. On screen, the cell divides into the icon's colours and converges onto its shape over 2.5 seconds, drawn by a WebGL2 shader in a worker. Turn off "Animate new work's icon", or ask your system for reduced motion, and the icon appears at once.

### It finds its place

Prioritizing then moves the row next to related work: into the section for its subject, or a new section when several rows share a subject no section fits. It waits until you've stepped away or the app is out of sight, so nothing moves while you're working.

### A bot is still a separate thing

A stem cell stays a task. Its agent can message bots but can't create one, so it never turns itself into a bot. When you want a bot, with its own face and one conversation that keeps going, choose New bot under the stem cell's line and pick a face, or ask the [Chief of Staff](/chief-of-staff/) to make one. The other kinds, such as a todo list or a document, are under the same line.

## What we don't know yet

Stem cells are experimental and off by default. Today a stem cell is always a task, and the other kinds still have their own buttons under its line. Whether one door is enough for everything is what we're finding out.

> Your interface needs one button to start work and one input box. That's it.

## Turn it on

In [Happy Desktop](/#download), open Settings → Experimental and turn on Spaces sidebar and Stem cells.
