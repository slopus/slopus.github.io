# New Work Starts as a Stem Cell

New work in Happy Desktop starts as one stem cell. Say what it is, and it names itself and grows into its icon.

## Eight ways to start

New task, +, New tab, New window, New chat, New todo list, New bot, New document. Each one asks what the thing is before you have said a word about it.

## One stem cell

Create in the sidebar, ⌘N and ⌘T all make the same thing: a row labelled "New" near the top of the sidebar. Its chat says "Say what it is — it'll find its place." A new bot or document is still one click away, under that line.

Type `ping me on serious posthog alerts` and send it. The row becomes "PostHog alerts", and the cell grows into the alerting icon.

## How it names itself

The chat's own model names the row by its subject in one to three words, at the model's default effort, within a minute. With your first message, Prioritizing, the agent that keeps your sidebar, picks the row's icon from 136 clay icons and sets it right away. It moves the row next to related work later, once you've stepped away.

When the icon arrives, the cell divides into the icon's colours and converges onto its silhouette over 2.5 seconds, drawn by a WebGL2 shader in a worker, then hands over to the plain image. Turn off "Animate new work's icon", or ask your system for reduced motion, and the icon appears at once.

## Turn it on

Stem cells are experimental. In [Happy Desktop](/#download), open Settings → Experimental and turn on Spaces sidebar and Stem cells.
