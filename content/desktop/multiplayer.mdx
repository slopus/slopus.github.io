# Multiplayer

One place for you, your team, and your agents. Everyone signs in as themselves and sees what the team and its agents are working on, with Claude, Codex, and Grok, on a server your team runs.

## See who's working on what

![Happy Desktop on the Happy Core team server, close on the sidebar. Under Tasks, each task carries its owner's photo, and teammates' new tasks, Launch Email and Docs Search, arrive at the top of the list as they start.](/video/multiplayer/aware/ "frame loop")

Every task on the team server is in your sidebar with its owner's face on it. When a teammate starts one, it shows up in your list as it starts, and you can open it and read along.

## Catch up on what shipped

![Happy Desktop's Activity page on the team server, with 3 unread. Under Today, each entry shows what it is about and who did it: three teammates' new tasks marked Created, and a web page an agent published, Onboarding email drafts, marked Created and 1 new version.](/video/multiplayer/activity/ "frame")

Activity, in Desktop's sidebar, is your list of what the team and its agents made, day by day: new tasks, bots, todo lists, documents, and artifacts. Changes you haven't looked at fold into one entry, and Following narrows it to what you follow.

## Shared bots for the standing jobs

![The team server's Desktop Releases bot open in Happy Desktop. A teammate writes: Tell me when it's out and I'll post the changelog. The bot answers her by name: @Anna, will do. macOS is signed and notarized. Windows is still building. It then waits 20 minutes for the Windows build.](/video/multiplayer/bots/ "frame")

A bot is one conversation that never ends, with a name, a picture, and a folder of its own. On a team server every member sees the same bots and writes into the same conversation, so a standing job has one place to live.

On our team server we run Desktop & Agent Releases, Mobile Releases, Growth Hacking, a Digital Ocean Watcher, and Product Health. Any member can start one from New bot in the sidebar.

## Share what your agents make

![An artifact open in Happy Desktop: a web page titled Cold sync benchmark, with a table of timings for 10k tasks (task list query 1.21 s, bots and Surfaces 0.34 s, render 0.25 s, total 1.80 s), and History, Add to sidebar, and Delete above it.](/video/multiplayer/artifacts/ "frame")

When an agent finishes a page, a report, or an image, it publishes it as an artifact. Everyone on the team server sees every artifact, wherever it was made, and every update keeps the earlier version.

## Step in when it's your call

![A task's session in Happy Desktop. A subtask reports it has finished, and the agent asks: @Kirill, should team servers use the same account as the desktop, or their own? The answer below reads: Same account. The agent replies: Same account it is. Wiring the desktop's sign-in into it now.](/video/multiplayer/step-in/ "frame")

Mostly you watch. When the work needs a decision that is yours, the task's agent asks you by name, in the session. Your teammates see your answer under your name and photo, and the agent is told who wrote, so it answers you.

**Away from your desk?** Pair your own phone with the team server. Read along, or answer when an agent asks for you; what you send arrives under your name, as it does from Desktop. See [Mobile Access](/mobile/).

## Shared todo lists, documents, and calendars

The team also keeps todo lists, documents, and calendars on the server. A new one is in every member's sidebar, and agents read and edit them too.

## Run your own team server

A team server is Happy Agent on a Linux machine or a Mac you control, switched into team mode. The Chief of Staff deploys it from a public recipe and asks you only for what is yours to decide.

Invitations go by email: ask the Chief of Staff to send one. Your teammate accepts, connects their Happy account in Desktop with that address, and asks their own Chief of Staff to connect them to the team.

```chief-of-staff add teammates
Invite bob@example.com to the Acme team
Connect me to the Acme team
```

[Team Server](/guides/team-server/) covers sign-in, how Desktop and phones reach the server, whose provider accounts pay, the sandbox, the owner's role, and what we haven't built yet.
