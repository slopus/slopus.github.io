# Multiplayer

One place for you, your team, and your agents. Everyone signs in as themselves and sees what the team and its agents are working on, with Claude, Codex, and Grok, on a server your team runs.

## See who's working on what

![Steve's phone, showing the team server's task list. The task Kirill just started on his desktop, Voice waveform, arrives at the top of the list with Kirill's photo on it, above Android Push Fixes, Activity Feed Design, and Team Servers, each with its owner's picture.](/video/multiplayer/aware/ "loop")

Every task on the team server is in your sidebar with its owner's face on it. When a teammate on your project starts one, it shows up in your list as it starts, and you can open it and read along.

Activity, in Desktop's sidebar, lists what your team and its agents made, day by day: new tasks, bots, todo lists, documents, and artifacts.

What you are still typing stays yours, and so does the order of your own list.

## Shared bots for the standing jobs

![The Bots section of a team server's sidebar in Happy Desktop: Chief of Staff, Disk Sweeper, iOS Production Train, and Product Health, each with its own picture. Product Health has a subtask running under it, Docs rage clicks.](/multiplayer/bots-sidebar.webp)

A bot is one conversation that never ends, with a name, a picture, and a folder of its own. On a team server every member sees the same bots and writes into the same conversation, so a standing job has one place to live.

On our team server we run Desktop & Agent Releases, Mobile Releases, Growth Hacking, a Digital Ocean Watcher, and Product Health. Start one from New bot in the team server's sidebar, or ask its Chief of Staff, the bot every team server starts with.

```chief-of-staff add a shared bot
Create a bot called Releases that owns our weekly desktop release
```

## Step in when it's your call

![A session on Steve's phone, in the task Kirill started. The agent ends its message with: Steve, how does it look? Steve types Love it. Narrower though, five bars max. and sends it; the message appears in the session.](/video/multiplayer/step-in/ "loop")

Mostly you watch. When the work needs a decision that is yours, the task's agent asks you by name, in the session. Answer from Desktop or from your own paired phone: your message lands under your name and photo, and the agent is told who wrote, so it answers you. See [Mobile Access](/mobile/).

## Shared todo lists and docs

![Placeholder for the screenshot of a shared todo list and a shared document.](/video/multiplayer/surfaces/ "loop")

Beside the agents, the team keeps todo lists, documents, and calendars. A new one shows up in every member's sidebar, and agents read and edit them too: ask one to tick off a todo or to add the release to the team calendar. Todos, documents, and calendar events keep every earlier version.

## Invite a teammate

Invitations go by email, and the Chief of Staff sends them; the app has no invite button. Your teammate accepts, connects their Happy account in Desktop with that address, and asks their own Chief of Staff to connect them to the team.

Where they are better: there is no server to run, and ChatGPT shares a project from a link on its free plan. Claude and ChatGPT invite from a Share button, ChatGPT's project owner can remove a member, and Claude's Team plan adds single sign-on and central billing.

## Run your own team server

A team server is Happy Agent on a Linux machine or a Mac you control, switched into team mode. The Chief of Staff deploys it from a public recipe and asks you only for what is yours to decide.

[Team Server](/guides/team-server/) covers sign-in, how Desktop and phones reach the server, whose provider accounts pay, the sandbox, the owner's role, and what we haven't built yet.
