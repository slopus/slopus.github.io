# Multiplayer

One place for you, your team, and your agents. Everyone signs in as themselves and works in the same tasks and sessions, with Claude, Codex, and Grok, on a server your team runs.

## Everyone sees the same work

![Steve's phone, showing the team server's task list. The task Kirill just sent from his desktop, Voice waveform, arrives at the top of the list with Kirill's photo on it, above Android Push Fixes and Activity Feed Design.](/video/multiplayer/same-work/ "loop")

Send a task and it is in your teammates' sidebars at once, with your face on it, on their desktops and their phones. Everyone on the team sees the same projects, tasks, and sessions.

What you are still typing stays yours, and so does the order of your own list.

## Agents ask the right teammate

![A session on Kirill's desktop. The agent posts a live preview of the voice waveform it built, then writes: Here's the waveform: the bars move while either side is speaking and rest flat when it's quiet. @Steve, how does it look?](/video/multiplayer/ask-teammate/ "loop")

A task's agent can see who is on the team. When the work needs someone, it asks them by name, in the session everyone is reading. A teammate who had left the task, or who turned off joining every new task, gets it back in their list.

## Anyone can steer, from their phone

![The same session on Steve's phone. Under the agent's question, Steve, how does it look?, he types Love it. Narrower though, five bars max. and sends it; the message appears in the session.](/video/multiplayer/steer/ "loop")

Any member can open a running session and write to the agent, from Desktop or from their own paired iPhone or Android phone. The message lands in the session under their name and photo, and the agent is told who is writing now, so it answers the person who asked. See [Mobile Access](/mobile/).

## Shared todo lists and docs

![Placeholder for the recording of a shared todo list and a shared document.](/video/multiplayer/surfaces/ "loop")

Beside the agents, the team keeps todo lists, documents, and calendars. A new one shows up in every member's sidebar.

Agents read and edit them too. Ask one to add the release to the team calendar or to tick off a todo. Todos, documents, and calendar events keep every earlier version.

## Invite a teammate

Invitations go by email, and the Chief of Staff sends them; the app has no invite button. Your teammate accepts, connects their Happy account in Desktop with that address, and asks their own Chief of Staff to connect them to the team.

## Run your own team server

A team server is Happy Agent on a Linux machine or a Mac you control, switched into team mode. The Chief of Staff deploys it from a public recipe and asks you only for what is yours to decide.

[Team Server](/guides/team-server/) covers sign-in, how Desktop and phones reach the server, whose provider accounts pay, the sandbox, the owner's role, and what we haven't built yet.
