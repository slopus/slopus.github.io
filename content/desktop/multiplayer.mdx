## See who's working on what

Every task on the team server is in your sidebar with its owner's face. A teammate's new task appears at the top as it starts. Open it to read along, then go back to your own. Leave a teammate's task you're not interested in, and it drops out of your sidebar. Tasks run on Claude, Codex, Grok, and more, using the subscriptions signed in on the server.

## See the most important updates from the team

When a teammate's agent publishes a review, a report, a design, or a screenshot, it arrives in Activity as an artifact card with a summary and a cover that previews it. Each new version folds into the same card. New tasks and shared lists are there too, grouped by day, with faces. An entry stays unread until you open it or mark all read.

For recurring work like releases, a shared bot helps own an area of your product, such as growth or product health. Every member sees the same bots and writes into the same conversation. Each bot keeps a conversation, a name, a picture, and a folder of its own.

On our team server we run Desktop & Agent Releases, Mobile Releases, Growth Hacking, a Digital Ocean Watcher, and Product Health. Any member can start one from New bot in the sidebar. Bots publish their work into Activity too.

## Also

**Step in when it's your call.** When the decision is yours, the agent asks you by name. Your teammates see your answer under your name and photo.

**On your phone.** Pair your phone with the team server to read Activity and tasks, or to answer an agent. See [Mobile Access](/mobile/).

**Shared todo lists, documents, and calendars.** They live on the server, appear in every member's sidebar, and agents read and edit them too.

## Run your own team server

A team server is Happy Agent on a Linux machine or a Mac you control, switched into team mode. The Chief of Staff deploys it from a public recipe and asks you only for what is yours to decide.

```chief-of-staff start a team
Create a team called Acme on my server ubuntu@203.0.113.10
```

Invitations go by email: ask the Chief of Staff to send one. Your teammate accepts, connects their Happy account in Desktop with that address, and asks their own Chief of Staff to connect them to the team.

```chief-of-staff add teammates
Invite bob@example.com to the Acme team
Connect me to the Acme team
```

[Team Server](/guides/team-server/) covers sign-in, how Desktop and phones reach the server, whose provider accounts pay, the sandbox, the owner's role, and what we haven't built yet.
