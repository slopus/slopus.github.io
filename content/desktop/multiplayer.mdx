# Multiplayer

One place for you, your team, and your agents. Everyone signs in as themselves and works in the same tasks and sessions, with Claude, Codex, and Grok, on a server your team runs.

## Work in the same session as your teammates

Everyone on the team sees the same projects, tasks, and sessions. A task you start appears in your teammates' sidebars as you send it, with your face on it. Any of them can open it and write to the agent while it works.

What you are still typing stays yours, and so does the order of your own list.

## The agent knows who is talking

Every message in a shared session carries its author's name and photo. When the speaker changes, the agent is told who is writing now, by name and email, so it answers the person who asked.

## Agents bring in the right teammate

A task's agent can see who is on the team. Ask it to bring someone in, or let it decide when the work concerns them: it adds that teammate to the task, and the task appears in their list.

## Shared todo lists, documents, calendars, and chats

Beside the agents, the team keeps Surfaces: todo lists, documents, calendars, and chats among people. A new one shows up in every member's sidebar.

Agents read and edit the same todo lists, documents, and calendars you do. Ask one to add the release to the team calendar or to tick off a todo. Todos, documents, and calendar events keep every earlier version.

## Every member has their own phone

Each member pairs their own iPhone or Android phone with the team server. From it they follow the team's sessions, send messages, answer an agent's questions, and stop a turn, end-to-end encrypted. See [Mobile Access](/mobile/).

## Invite a teammate

Invitations go by email, and the Chief of Staff sends them; the app has no invite button. Your teammate accepts, connects their Happy account in Desktop with that address, and asks their own Chief of Staff to connect them to the team.

## Run your own team server

A team server is Happy Agent on a Linux machine or a Mac you control, switched into team mode. The Chief of Staff deploys it from a public recipe and asks you only for what is yours to decide.

[Team Server](/guides/team-server/) covers sign-in, how Desktop and phones reach the server, whose provider accounts pay, the sandbox, the owner's role, and what we haven't built yet.
