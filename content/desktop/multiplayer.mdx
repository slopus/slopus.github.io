# Multiplayer

One place for you, your team, and your agents. Everyone signs in as themselves and sees what the team and its agents are working on, with Claude, Codex, and Grok, on a server your team runs.

## Shared bots for the standing jobs

![The Happy Core team server in Happy Desktop, with the bot Desktop Releases open. The sidebar lists the team's bots: Chief of Staff, Desktop Releases, Mobile Releases, Alerts, and Growth. In the bot's one conversation, Steve writes: Cut a preview once main is green. The bot answers that it is cutting the preview. Anna writes: Tell me when it's out and I'll post the changelog. The bot answers: Will do, @Anna. macOS is signed and notarized. Windows is still building.](/video/multiplayer/bots/ "window 344x390")

A bot is one conversation that never ends, with a name, a picture, and a folder of its own. On a team server every member sees the same bots and writes into the same conversation, so a standing job has one place to live.

On our team server we run Desktop & Agent Releases, Mobile Releases, Growth Hacking, a Digital Ocean Watcher, and Product Health. Any member can start one from New bot in the sidebar.

## Step in when it's your call

![A task's session in Happy Desktop. The agent writes: The waveform is in, on one row at the mute button's height. @Steve, five bars or seven on the phone? Under Steve's name and picture: Five. Seven crowds the mute button. The agent answers: Five bars it is.](/video/multiplayer/step-in/ "region 789x345 420x415")

Mostly you watch. When the work needs a decision that is yours, the task's agent asks you by name, in the session. Your answer lands under your name and photo, and the agent is told who wrote, so it answers you.

![Stand-in for this screenshot. Happy on a phone, connected to a server named Happy Core. It lists two bots, Chief of Staff and iOS Production Train, and under them the tasks: Voice waveform at the top with Kirill's photo, then Android Push Fixes, Activity Feed Design, and Team Servers.](/video/multiplayer/phone/ "phone")

**Away from your desk?** Pair your own phone with the team server. Read along, or answer when an agent asks for you; what you send arrives under your name, as it does from Desktop. See [Mobile Access](/mobile/).

## Shared todo lists, documents, and calendars

The team also keeps todo lists, documents, and calendars on the server. A new one is in every member's sidebar, and agents read and edit them too. Activity lists what the team and its agents made, day by day.

## Run your own team server

A team server is Happy Agent on a Linux machine or a Mac you control, switched into team mode. The Chief of Staff deploys it from a public recipe and asks you only for what is yours to decide.

Invitations go by email: ask the Chief of Staff to send one. Your teammate accepts, connects their Happy account in Desktop with that address, and asks their own Chief of Staff to connect them to the team.

```chief-of-staff add teammates and bots
Invite bob@example.com to the Acme team
Connect me to the Acme team
Create a bot called Releases that owns our weekly desktop release
```

[Team Server](/guides/team-server/) covers sign-in, how Desktop and phones reach the server, whose provider accounts pay, the sandbox, the owner's role, and what we haven't built yet.
