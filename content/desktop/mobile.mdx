# Mobile Access

Pair the [Happy app for iPhone or Android](/mobile-app/) once, and every session on your computer is on your phone, end-to-end encrypted. The agent stays on your machine, with your files and your permission boundary.

```chief-of-staff pair your phone
Pair my phone
```

## Pair your phone with Happy Desktop

**Settings → Mobile Access**, or during onboarding:

1. Install **Happy** from the App Store or Google Play. Desktop shows a QR code for the listing.
2. Scan the **device QR** Desktop shows next and approve the computer on the phone. One scan.

An already-linked computer is reused without another scan.

**Already using the [original Happy CLI](/docs/)?** Your account and sessions keep working. Connect Desktop from **Settings → Mobile Access**; the CLI and Desktop appear on the phone as two machines on the same account.

## How the phone sync is encrypted

The phone holds the account's primary key. Content is encrypted on one device and decrypted on another. The relay stores and forwards opaque blobs; it sees that machines and sessions exist and when they were active, not what is in them. Full write-up: [Security & Encryption](/docs/security/).

## What you can do from the phone

- Send messages, with encrypted images.
- Answer permission requests and questions. A phone reply is a trusted answer.
- Stop the turn.
- Switch model and effort. Change the permission mode.
- Start a session in a directory. A directory that does not exist becomes a request to create it, never a silent `mkdir`.
- Read files and the session's Git changes through the same boundary the agent has.

## Troubleshooting mobile access

- Never delete or reset `~/.happy`. It holds history, configuration, and account state.
- A saved link can be offline; Desktop says "connected" only on live status.
- A lost phone or a conflicting saved account is a recovery conversation. Keep both sides until you choose.
