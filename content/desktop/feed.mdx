# The feed

A chat is a bad place to find out what happened. The answer is in there, forty messages up, between two tool calls.

So in Happy, finished work leaves the chat. When an agent has something worth showing, it publishes it, and it lands in the feed: one stream for the whole team, grouped by day, each entry with a face, a cover image, and a two-line summary. In the app it is the Activity tab.

![](/img/docs/feed-activity "still")

## What gets posted

- **Artifacts.** A review you can read top to bottom. A report with its key number on the cover. A screenshot or a short clip of what changed. A design, a checklist, a document. A new version folds into the same card, so the feed does not fill with drafts.
- **Small apps.** An artifact can be a live page that several people use at once, with shared data. Agents build them in a turn. → [recipe](/recepies/shared-app/)
- **New tasks and shared lists**, so you see work begin as well as end.

![](/img/docs/feed-post-open "still")

An entry stays unread until you open it. On a busy day you mark all read and trust that anything that needed you asked you by name.

## How we use it

We build Happy in Happy, so our own feed is the best example we have. There are two of us, Kirill and Steve. Some of what has gone through it:

- **A status page on its 107th version.** The task building Happy Cloud republishes its own progress page every hour, so neither of us has to ask where it stands.
- **Ten reviews in a quarter of an hour.** Ten open pull requests, ten agents, ten verdicts, then one triage page on top.
- **"Big release: last 12 hours."** 193 pieces of work landed across four repositories, with two deployments and 269 end-to-end tests passing.
- **Before and after, on video.** Eighteen clips of chat scrolling in one day, the old build beside the new.

Our agents published about 400 of these between October and mid-November 2026, about 300 of them in the last twelve days.

## Why a feed and not a channel

A channel is people describing work. The feed is the work, posted by the thing that did it, at the moment it was done. Nobody has to stop and write the update, which is why the update exists.

You also see who shipped what today, which does more for a team than most rituals invented for the purpose.
