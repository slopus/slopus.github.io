# Happy vs Cursor Projects, Claude Team, and Capy

Cursor, Anthropic, OpenAI, and Capy have all shipped a way for a team to share agent work. We think they are right about the idea. Happy differs in three ways: it is open source, it runs on computers you choose, and it uses the model subscriptions you already have, from several vendors at once.

What working together in Happy looks like is on [One place for the team](/multiplayer/).

## Where they are better

There is no server to run, and ChatGPT shares a project from a link on its free plan. Claude and ChatGPT invite from a Share button, ChatGPT's project owner can remove a member, and Claude's Team plan adds single sign-on and central billing.

Capy supplies cloud VMs and pull-request reviews without a per-seat charge.
